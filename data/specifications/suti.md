# Description

SUTI is a Swedish abbreviation that translates to standardized exchange of transport information in a demand responsive transport (DRT) context. In English SUTI can be read as Standardized Utilization of Transport Information. The aim is to develop and maintain a standard for communication between a Client and a Provider in a DRT environment.
